The engagement engine

We give people a reason to pull out their phone — then your offer is right there.

Getting the phone out of someone's pocket is 90% of the friction in physical advertising. The center of the board is designed to do exactly that, every week.

  1. The hub stays interesting

    A rotating mix of Coffee HAUS specials, weekly trivia on NYC, Murray Hill, and music, plus event notices and the guest Wi-Fi code — so regulars check the board every visit.

  2. A weekly hook gets the scan

    A "this week's pick" — an apartment hack or lifestyle find — gives even skeptics a reason to raise their camera. That's the hard part, solved.

  3. Your listing rides along

    Every time someone scans, the neighborhood-exclusives panel — your offer, your booking button — is displayed right beneath it on their screen. Verified local eyeballs, straight to your coupon.

  4. Your own plaque QR too

    Each plaque carries a dedicated QR routing to your booking link, Google reviews, or a custom landing page. Two paths to your door.
