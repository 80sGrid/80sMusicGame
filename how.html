🎮 The basics

  1. Each column is an artist and each row is a category.
  2. Tap a square and type the answer that fits both.
  3. Fill in as many of the nine squares as you can, then tap Check Answers to see your score.

💡 Good to know

  • ✅Often more than one answer works.
  • 🎯Stuck? You get 3 lifelines per game. Each one turns a square into multiple choice.
  • ⭐Every grid has a difficulty rating: Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • 🆕A brand new game drops every day. Play daily to build your streak.
  • 🏆Score 6 or more to unlock a bonus grid, and see how you rank on the leaderboard.
  • 🎶Want more? There are three themed quizzes every week too.
